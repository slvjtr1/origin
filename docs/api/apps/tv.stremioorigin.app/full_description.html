Origin: a native app for Stremio addons on LG webOS TVs (ARM), in English or Arabic.

Bring your own Stremio account and addons: the app ships empty and plays only the links your addons return.

  • A new player bar: Three groups of icons: the picture on the left (upscale, picture size), the film in the middle (back, play, forward, with your seek step written on them), sound and subtitles on the right.
  • OpenSubtitles Pro, built in: More subtitles and none of the spam. Choose in Settings: with AI translations, made by people only, or off.
  • Brightness and See in the dark, stronger: Dark scenes are opened up properly now, and both work on Google TV too, with Clear voice and Even volume.
  • Reduce for old TVs: Google TV: a lighter app for slow devices. On by itself where memory is short, or a switch in Settings, Appearance.

Unofficial: not affiliated with Stremio. Updates install from inside the app (Settings › About).
