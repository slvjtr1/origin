Origin: a native app for Stremio addons on LG webOS TVs (ARM), in English or Arabic.

Bring your own Stremio account and addons: the app ships empty and plays only the links your addons return.

  • Steadier Sources: A fault that could close the app when many addons answered at once, as on the first press of Sources, is fixed.
  • Lighter on your TV: Memory is kept within bounds while addons answer, and handed back without stalling the picture.
  • Plug in a drive, that is all: The USB check stops cleanly when the drive is pulled out, touches only its own files, and OK hides it.

Unofficial: not affiliated with Stremio. Updates install from inside the app (Settings › About).
