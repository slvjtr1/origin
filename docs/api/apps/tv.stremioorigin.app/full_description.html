Origin: a native app for Stremio addons on LG webOS TVs (ARM).

Bring your own Stremio account and addons: the app ships empty and plays only the links your addons return.

  • Resume at a glance: The Play button says what it plays and how much is left, with a ring showing how far you got.
  • Source health: A coloured mark on each stream: smooth, may buffer or slow. Turn it on in Settings > Playback.
  • Playback info: Press the blue key while a film plays: the source, the file, its bitrate and a live graph of the buffer.

Unofficial: not affiliated with Stremio. Updates install from inside the app (Settings › About).
