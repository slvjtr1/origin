Origin: a native app for Stremio addons on LG webOS TVs (ARM), in English or Arabic.

Bring your own Stremio account and addons: the app ships empty and plays only the links your addons return.

  • Super clean sources: Sources that are really another title (a spin-off, a sequel, a film of another year), another episode or a cinema recording are left out before you see them. The last row of the list says how many, and shows them if you ask.
  • The wrong video never comes back: When a source plays something else than what you opened, the player notices by itself and moves to the next one. You can also say so from the sources list: that source stays out for good.
  • Names stay in the subtitles: A name written between brackets or quotes is no longer lost with the marks around it. Lines that advertise a site or credit the translators are hidden.
  • Old Arabic subtitles read right: Subtitle files and built-in tracks saved in the old Arabic encoding now show as Arabic, not as symbols.

Unofficial: not affiliated with Stremio. Updates install from inside the app (Settings › About).
