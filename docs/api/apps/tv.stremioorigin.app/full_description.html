Origin: a native app for Stremio addons on LG webOS TVs (ARM), in English or Arabic.

Bring your own Stremio account and addons: the app ships empty and plays only the links your addons return.

  • Lighter on your TV: ORIGIN hands memory back to the TV at once: when a film starts, when you leave it, and when you press Home.
  • Plug in a drive, that is all: A new USB drive is checked in seconds and torrents buffer on it by themselves. An SSD works best.
  • Your subtitle languages, every time: Found whatever way an addon names the language. Your second language steps in when the first has none.

Unofficial: not affiliated with Stremio. Updates install from inside the app (Settings › About).
