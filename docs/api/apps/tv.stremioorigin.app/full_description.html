Origin: a native app for Stremio addons on LG webOS TVs (ARM), in English or Arabic.

Bring your own Stremio account and addons: the app ships empty and plays only the links your addons return.

  • ORIGIN 1.0: A media center for your addons, on LG webOS and Google TV: your library, the best stream in one press, and subtitles that stay in sync.
  • A source fails, the next one plays: When a source does not play, the next best one starts by itself, then a third. If all three fail you choose from them.
  • Sound on every device: On Google TV the app carries its own decoders for Dolby, DTS and TrueHD: older boxes without them play every film with sound.
  • Clean sources, the right episode: Fake files and sources of another episode are left out, and a torrent pack plays the episode you opened.

Unofficial: not affiliated with Stremio. Updates install from inside the app (Settings › About).
