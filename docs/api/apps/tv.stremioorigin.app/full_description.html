Origin: a native app for Stremio addons on LG webOS TVs (ARM), in English or Arabic.

Bring your own Stremio account and addons: the app ships empty and plays only the links your addons return.

  • Only your subtitle languages: Choose a default and a second subtitle language, Arabic and English for example: subtitles in other languages are left out, from your addons and OpenSubtitles alike. Settings, Subtitles.
  • English only, for now: ORIGIN is in English for the time being. We are sorry to everyone who used it in Arabic: Arabic caused big problems in building the app. Arabic subtitles still work fully.

Unofficial: not affiliated with Stremio. Updates install from inside the app (Settings › About).
