Origin: a native app for Stremio addons on LG webOS TVs (ARM).

Bring your own Stremio account and addons: the app ships empty and plays only the links your addons return.

  • Live TV: Channels and sports from your live addons, with Favorites and Recent.
  • Trakt: What you watch tracked, watched marks, Continue Watching and Trakt rows on Home. Bring your own Client ID.
  • Seek previews: A picture of the film on the seek bar as you move through it, for debrid and direct links.
  • Chapters: The film's chapters on the seek bar and in the seek pill. Settings › Seek bar.
  • Tracks per show: The subtitles and audio you pick stay for the whole show.

Unofficial: not affiliated with Stremio. Updates install from inside the app (Settings › About).
