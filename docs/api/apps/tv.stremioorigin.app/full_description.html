Origin: a native app for Stremio addons on LG webOS TVs (ARM).

Bring your own Stremio account and addons: the app ships empty and plays only the links your addons return.

  • Sleep timer: The moon button in the player: 15 to 90 minutes or the episode’s end. The film then pauses, your place kept, and the screen goes dark.
  • Steadier on a busy TV: When the TV runs short of memory, subtitles, previews and pictures now make do instead of closing the app.
  • Seek previews: A stream that drops while its previews are being made no longer closes the app.

Unofficial: not affiliated with Stremio. Updates install from inside the app (Settings › About).
