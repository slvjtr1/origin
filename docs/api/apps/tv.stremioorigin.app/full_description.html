Origin: a native app for Stremio addons on LG webOS TVs (ARM).

Bring your own Stremio account and addons: the app ships empty and plays only the links your addons return.

  • Account switching: Trakt activity, profiles and upcoming episode dismissals stay with your account.
  • Playback preferences: Subtitle requests follow the current title. Forced subtitles and per-show audio choices work across more sources.
  • Discover and search: Browse longer catalogs and see available search results while slower sources respond.
  • Film collections: Collection pages recover from delayed title lookups and finish loading when a source is unavailable.

Unofficial: not affiliated with Stremio. Updates install from inside the app (Settings › About).
